**Why this one wins:**

It came up in every single interview i.e. not just one or two people, all 5.

It happens almost every day for each of them, not occasionally.

It causes real pain i.e headaches, back strain, even needing medicine in one case.

These are easy users to find and test with — the same audience we already interviewed.

**A strong signal from the research:**
